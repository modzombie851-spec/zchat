Dead Town sound credits

All sounds were trimmed, mixed to mono and level matched for the game.

Gunshots, the sniper bolt, shotgun shell loading and the empty click
  Snake's Authentic Gun Sounds, packs one and two, by Snake (F8 Studios), f8studios.itch.io
  Free for commercial use. Credit is not required, but we are happy to give it.

Rifle, pistol and shotgun reloads
  Supplied by the ZChat owner for the game.

Zombie screams and groans
  Free zombie sound effects pack by EditingCorp, editingcorp.com (royalty free)

Footsteps, hits, glass, slime, fire, slashes, menu clicks and wave sounds
  Kenney impact, interface, UI, RPG, digital and sci fi sound packs, kenney.nl (CC0)

Explosions, the game over sound, wind, night crickets, water and the car engine
  Mixkit sound effects, mixkit.co (Mixkit free license)
